# Tourist VAT Refund

## Who Is Eligible

Visitors who live outside Turkey can claim a VAT refund on purchases made in KargoShop pickup stores. The purchase must total at least 1,000 TL on a single invoice.

## How to Claim

Show your passport at the store counter when paying and ask for a tax-free form. The form must be stamped by customs at the airport when you leave the country within 3 months of the purchase.

## Refund Timing

Approved refunds are paid to your card within 14 days after the stamped form reaches our refund partner. Online orders delivered to Turkish addresses are not eligible.
