# Warranty for International Buyers

## Where the Warranty Applies

The manufacturer warranty is valid only inside Turkey. Products taken abroad can only be repaired if they are sent back to Turkey.

## Service Centers

Authorized service centers operate in 12 Turkish cities. The list is shown on your warranty request page after you submit a claim.

## Shipping Costs

For buyers with a non-Turkish address, the cost of shipping the product to the service center is paid by the customer. Return shipping within Turkey after repair is free.
