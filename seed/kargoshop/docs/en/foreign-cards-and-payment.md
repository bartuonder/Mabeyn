# Paying with Foreign Cards

## Accepted Cards

Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards issued outside Turkey are accepted. American Express and Diners Club are not accepted.

## Currency and Fees

All prices are in Turkish lira. Your bank converts the amount to your own currency and may add a foreign transaction fee. KargoShop does not charge extra for foreign cards.

## Installments

Installment plans are only available for cards issued by partner banks in Turkey. Foreign cards are always charged in a single payment.

## Failed Payments

If a foreign card payment is declined, check that online international payments are enabled by your bank and that 3D Secure verification is active.
