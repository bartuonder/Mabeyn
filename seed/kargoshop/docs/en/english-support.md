# English Language Support

## Support Hours

English support is available Monday to Friday between 10:00 and 17:00 Turkey time. The team is closed on weekends and public holidays.

## Channels

Write to english@kargoshop.example or use the English live chat on the website. Phone support in English is not available.

## Response Times

Emails in English are answered within 2 business days. Live chat usually responds within 5 minutes during support hours.
