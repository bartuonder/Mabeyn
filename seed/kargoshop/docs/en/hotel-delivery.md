# Delivery to Hotels

## Where We Deliver

Orders can be delivered to hotel reception desks in Istanbul, Ankara and Izmir. Express delivery is recommended so the package arrives during your stay.

## Required Details

Put your full name, your room number and the hotel phone number in the address notes. The courier will ask the reception to confirm that you are a guest.

## Missed Deliveries

If the guest has checked out, the package is returned to our warehouse and the payment is refunded. We cannot redirect a package to another hotel once it has shipped.
