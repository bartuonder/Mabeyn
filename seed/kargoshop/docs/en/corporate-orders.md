# Corporate Orders

## Volume Discounts

Orders of 10 or more units of the same product receive an 8 percent discount. Orders of 50 or more units receive a custom quote from the sales team.

## Payment Terms

Approved business customers can pay by invoice within 30 days of delivery. Credit approval takes up to 5 business days and requires a tax certificate.

## Account Manager

Business customers with yearly purchases above 100,000 TL get a dedicated account manager who handles orders, returns and warranty claims.
